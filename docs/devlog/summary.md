# Devlog Summary

- 2026-11-11: **Snow and rain reach the walls, and stay off the people** — settled snow stopped in a hard green rectangle under every roof's one-block overhang, because the same column map that stops falling rain decided where it lies; weather now drifts one block in under an eave. Creatures, loose bodies and the held item are marked in the G-buffer and take no snow or puddles. Found in a winter capture of the village; no gate reads it.

- 2026-11-11: **The island is lived in** — the village simulation (62 residents with names, needs, households and sixteen trades; a daily and weekly routine; an economy of 22 goods with posted prices and exactly conserved coin; A* pathfinding with doors and gates) and the wildlife simulation (ten wild species with herds, warrens, a wolf pack, flocks and fish stocks; plants that spread, ripen and are grazed) are merged and run in the game on one shared context, so hunters and fishers draw on the wildlife and foxes and wolves on the farm animals. Right-clicking a villager talks, or opens trade if they are serving; `L` shows the ledger with price histories and `K` the wildlife census with population curves. The held block is drawn in the player's hand, sunlight caustics move over lake and sea beds, and a twilight term keeps dusk lit (single scattering left a sunset orange with black shadows). In the browser gate, half a game hour moves 48 of the 62 people and 234 of 353 wild animals with no villager teleported, at 5.3 ms a frame from the air and 5.1 ms in the street. A woodcutter's tree is removed whole rather than felled through physics, so its logs are counted once. Wildlife is proven stable for three years on the default seed only; other seeds lean on immigration, and a follow-up is running.

- 2026-11-11: **Things fall, float, spill and collapse in the game** — the physics layer (finite water that conserves volume, sand and gravel that fall and heap, structures that come down when their support goes, Rapier rigid bodies on per-chunk voxel colliders) is merged and stepped by the game in real time at 0.1 to 0.3 ms a frame. A body is drawn with the block's own tiles and boxes, so a felled trunk or a piece of rubble keeps the face it had in the grid. Breaking a block now drops an item that lies where it lands until the player walks over it; rubble lying out of square can be hit to break it into its blocks; splashes, impacts, collapses and bursting leaves throw particles. In the browser gate a stone let go falls 0.448 m in 0.3 s (0.441 m predicted) and comes to rest, sand dropped in the air lands, and three loaves at the player's feet end up in the pack. Spilt water first drew as solid white: thin water is shallow everywhere and the shader took that for a shoreline, so the mesher now marks it. The physics library is 4.4 MB and loads in its own chunk while the island generates.

- 2026-11-11: **Mist at dawn, beams through it, and chips off a broken block** — still, clear nights now leave mist that burns off within a few hours of sunrise (about four mornings in five), and fog takes light out of the sun's beam and returns it as soft fill, so thick fog has no shadows and covers the sky. A half-resolution pass marches each view ray through the shadow cascades to draw the light the mist scatters, shadowed by trees, roofs and cloud. Breaking a block throws chips that fall, bounce and are lit like everything else. Looking closely at a birch trunk showed ambient occlusion smearing dark halos round high-contrast texels: it was reading the painted relief normals, and now rebuilds the face normal from depth. Leaves were repainted (the old tile repeated as rings) and timber lightened from near black. `scripts/capture.mjs` saves full-resolution frames headlessly for inspection, because the desktop pane's screenshots crop when hidden. The browser gate now stops the game's own loop before stepping it (the walk test failed once under load), and loads the full island: 25 buildings, 3.5 ms a frame from the air, 2.6 ms in the street at 1280 x 720. Villagers, animals and physics are still not in the game.

- 2026-11-11: **A village stands on the island, under real weather** — world generation now lays out a village on a levelled shelf: a paved square with a well and four stalls, four graded roads with plank bridges, 25 half-timbered, stone and thatched buildings furnished for 62 residents, fenced fields and pens, street lamps, a pier and a quarry, all of it reachable on foot by the one walkability rule (checked by a flood fill in the tests). Above it are volumetric clouds that cast shadows, a weather chain (clear, fair, overcast, rain, storm, fog) with rain, snow, wet ground, puddles, snow cover and lightning, and exposure that follows the light so dusk and moonlit nights stay readable. Fifteen kinds of creature have box models with walk, work, sleep and flight poses, and the interface has a hotbar, pack, almanac, inspector and help. Nothing lives in the village yet: the simulations are being built separately against `src/sim/api.ts`.

- 2026-11-11: **There is an island to walk on** — a 768-block island (mountain, lake, river, forests, beaches) generates in 3.2 s in a worker and renders through a raw WebGL2 deferred pipeline: four shadow cascades, baked sky and torch light with ambient occlusion, a physically based sky lookup, refractive and reflective water with foam, bloom and filmic tone mapping. Every shader compiled first time; the first defects were visual (anisotropic filtering forces linear magnification on Direct3D, so crisp texels needed a dedicated sampler; a stone tile that read as tread plate; a dark band below the horizon). The player walks, jumps and swims under one 9.81 m/s² gravity. The browser gate reads real pixels and measures 0.53 ms a frame at 1280 x 720 on a 256-block island. No clouds, weather effects, village or wildlife on screen yet.

- 2026-11-11: **The repository has a base to build a game on** — toolchain and five gates (`npm run verify`), a 290-state block table with one Uint16 id per cell, sectioned world storage that allocates nothing for uniform sections, and voxel light whose incremental repair matches a full recompute exactly over 600 random edits. The owner's four decisions (standalone engine, explorer-builder, all four physics systems, bounded island) and the call to drop Three.js for raw WebGL2 are in the spec. Nothing is drawn yet beyond a cleared canvas.
